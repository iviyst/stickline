    Controls

    KEYBOARD
    A D or ← → run · Space W ↑ jump (hold for height) · S ↓ slide · Shift C K dash · J E attack · R back to checkpoint · Esc pause · M sound
    MOUSE
    Click every menu. Turn on Mouse steering in Options to play with it: the player runs toward your cursor, left click jumps, right click slides, middle click dashes (or use Shift).
    CONTROLLER
    Left stick or D-pad run · A jump · B slide · X attack · Y, bumpers or triggers dash · Start pause · Back restarts from the checkpoint. The D-pad and A also drive the menus.
    TOUCH
    Two floating joysticks, no buttons. Left side: press and drag to run; the further you push, the faster you go. Right side: touch to jump (keep holding for a higher jump or to glide), swipe down to slide, flick sideways to dash. Quick tap on the left side: attack. One thumb works too: push the left stick far up to jump or far down to slide.
    MOVES
    Wall-jump: jump at a wall, press jump again; push toward the wall to hop up it, away to kick off. Dash: one per air time, refilled by the ground, walls and crystals. Hyper jump: jump while dashing on the ground. Slide jump: jump out of a slide for extra distance. Orbs: press jump inside one for a big boost. Grab a hang glider, then hold jump to glide. Ledge catch: fall past a ledge to grab it, then jump or push toward it to climb. Crouch: hold slide while standing still. Water: press jump to swim up; the bubbles over your head are your breath. Red light: freeze while it glares. VIP cards open VIP doors.
    HEAD TO HEAD
    Race a CPU or someone online over the same course. Hit them with your weapon to knock them back and stun them. First to the exit wins. Buy weapons with gems and skins with coins in the Store.
    GOALS
    Three stars per act: clear it, collect all 3 gems, beat the par time. Every act also hides a secret trophy behind a wall that isn't really there. Gems unlock new looks and buy weapons; coins buy skins.
